Open positions

Come work with us

We are looking for motivated M.S. (Research) and PhD students to tackle challenging questions about the role of the ocean & cryosphere in climate.

Who we're looking for

A background in fluid dynamics, thermodynamics, oceanography, atmospheric or climate science, physics, mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science or AI/ML with some coding experience in Python/MATLAB/Julia.

How to apply

Apply through the IIT Delhi PG admissions portal, and feel free to email Prof. Saurabh Rathore at srathore@iitd.ac.in if you're interested in working with the lab.
